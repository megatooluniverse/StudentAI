Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Student AI Pro. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your data when you visit our website/application and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you. Our app is designed to be privacy-first, keeping as much data on your device as possible.

2. Local Storage & Data Collection

Unlike many other web applications, Student AI Pro does not require an account and does not store your personal information on our servers. We use your browser's local storage (Local Storage API) to save:

  • Your recent question history (to allow you to easily view past queries).
  • Your study analytics (number of questions asked, pomodoro sessions completed).
  • Your theme preferences (Light/Dark mode).

This data never leaves your device unless you manually clear your browser data or use the "Clear History" options provided in the app.

3. Device Permissions (Mic & Camera)

To provide advanced features, our application may request access to your device hardware. These features are strictly opt-in:

  • Microphone: Used exclusively for the "Voice Typing" feature. Speech-to-text processing is handled by your browser's native Web Speech API. We do not record or store your audio.
  • Camera / File Upload: Used for the OCR (Optical Character Recognition) feature. Image processing is done locally on your device or via secure, temporary processing to extract text. We do not store your uploaded images.

4. Third-Party AI Services

Student AI Pro acts as a "Super Prompt" generator. When you click the buttons to send your prompt to AI providers (ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude, Copilot), you are leaving our application and navigating to those respective third-party platforms.

Please be aware that your queries will be subject to the Privacy Policies of those specific companies (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Microsoft) once you paste and submit the prompt on their websites.

5. Children's Privacy

While our tool is built for students, we do not knowingly collect identifiable personal data from children under the age of 13. Because our app functions primarily on local storage without user accounts, no personal identification is transmitted to our servers.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
